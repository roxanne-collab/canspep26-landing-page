# Outside the Box BC CanSPEP 2026 Landing Page Brief

## Project Name

Outside the Box BC CanSPEP 2026 QR Landing Page

## Landing Page URL

canspep26.outsidetheboxbc.ca

## Project Context

Outside the Box BC is attending the 2026 CanSPEP Conference as a breakfast sponsor. The physical activation includes custom ice trays as the attendee gift. QR codes on the table cards or gift materials will send attendees to this landing page.

The landing page needs to do two jobs at once:

1. Give attendees the drink recipes connected to the custom ice trays.
2. Use the traffic from the recipes to clearly explain what Outside the Box BC is, why planners should remember it, and why they should visit the main Outside the Box BC website.

This page is not meant to be a full public SEO landing page. It is a hidden campaign page for QR traffic from the conference.

The priority is not recipe downloads, lead capture, newsletter signup, or a long sales funnel. The priority is brand recognition and message clarity.

The most important thing the visitor should understand:

Outside the Box BC helps meeting planners source creative, cost-conscious, and memorable BC meeting destinations beyond the usual big-city options. It connects planners with Kamloops, Penticton, and Nanaimo through one website, one place to start, and a multi-destination incentive worth asking about.

## Primary Goal

Help CanSPEP attendees understand what Outside the Box BC is in the least confusing way possible.

The landing page should reinforce these ideas repeatedly:

- One website.
- Three BC destinations.
- Kamloops, Penticton, and Nanaimo.
- Creative, cost-conscious, and memorable meeting destinations.
- Not another beige ballroom.
- Ask us about our multi-destination incentive.
- Visit Outside the Box BC.

## Secondary Goal

Provide easy, mobile-friendly drink recipes connected to the custom ice tray gift.

The recipes are the hook. The Outside the Box BC message is the point.

## Audience

The primary audience is CanSPEP attendees.

They are mostly independent event planner business owners who influence client destination decisions. They may also include corporate planners, association planners, incentive travel planners, suppliers, and other event industry stakeholders.

Write for planners who are busy, scanning quickly on their phones, and thinking about destination ideas they can bring back to clients.

They do not need a long explanation of what a meeting destination is. They need a clear reason to remember Outside the Box BC and a simple next step.

## Brand Positioning

Outside the Box BC is a collaboration between three BC meeting destinations:

- Kamloops
- Penticton
- Nanaimo

The page should explain Outside the Box BC like this:

Outside the Box BC helps planners source creative, cost-conscious, and memorable BC meeting destinations beyond the usual big-city options. Come see Kamloops, Penticton, and Nanaimo for yourself.

## Core Message

One website. Three BC destinations. Way less sourcing chaos.

Outside the Box BC brings Kamloops, Penticton, and Nanaimo together in one place so planners can explore three strong destination options without starting from scratch three separate times.

## Key Differentiators

Use these ideas throughout the page:

- One place to start for all three destinations.
- Creative alternatives to default big-city meeting choices.
- Cost-conscious destination options.
- Memorable delegate experiences.
- Destination teams who understand meetings and events.
- A multi-destination incentive worth asking about.
- Experiences that make client recommendations more interesting.
- The page should feel like an answer to planner problems: access, cost, experience, and client memorability.

## Main CTA

Use one primary CTA throughout the page:

Visit Outside the Box BC

CTA destination:

https://www.outsidetheboxbc.ca/

## Secondary CTA Language

Do not create multiple competing buttons unless needed for layout. Use this as supporting copy, not necessarily as a second button:

Ask us about our multi-destination incentive.

## Important CTA Rule

Do not make the page feel like a form funnel. The goal is to send attendees to the main Outside the Box BC website.

Do not gate the recipes.

Do not require email submission.

Do not push a downloadable PDF.

Do not add a newsletter signup unless explicitly requested later.

## Incentive Message

Use light, repeated incentive language.

Approved wording:

Ask us about our multi-destination incentive.

Optional supporting line:

Book beyond the obvious and ask how the multi-destination incentive can work when your meetings land in more than one Outside the Box BC destination.

Do not include full legal terms on this page.

Do not explain the full incentive terms in detail.

Do not add booking conditions unless requested.

The detailed incentive information already lives on the main Outside the Box BC website.

## Tone and Voice

Tone should be:

- Cheeky
- Confident
- Planner-aware
- Punchy
- Clear
- Slightly sassy
- Not corporate
- Not generic tourism brochure
- Not overly cute
- Not vague

Energy level:

Full "not another beige ballroom" energy.

Use cheeky lines, but never at the expense of clarity.

The page needs to be readable on a phone at breakfast.

## Words and Phrases to Avoid

Avoid:

- "Folks"
- Em dashes
- En dashes
- Corporate jargon
- "Hidden gem" unless used very carefully
- "Unforgettable" too many times
- "World-class" unless there is proof
- Generic destination marketing filler
- Overly formal sponsorship language
- Too many CTAs
- Too much legal language
- Too much incentive fine print
- Anything that makes this feel like a downloadable recipe blog instead of a campaign landing page
