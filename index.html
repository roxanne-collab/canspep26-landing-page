The Chill Loops Brew

A smooth, slightly indulgent iced coffee that feels a step up from basic.

Coffee ice cubes. Because watered-down coffee is not a strategy.

Cold brew, maple, cinnamon, and ice that knows how to pull its weight.

Ingredients

  • Cold brew or strong brewed coffee, cooled
  • Milk or oat milk
  • Maple syrup or vanilla syrup
  • Dash of cinnamon
  • Ice cubes
  • Optional: coffee ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice cubes. Coffee ice cubes are ideal if you want zero dilution.
  2. Pour in cold brew.
  3. Add a splash of milk or oat milk.
  4. Stir in 1 to 2 teaspoons of maple syrup or vanilla syrup.
  5. Finish with a light sprinkle of cinnamon.

Penticton Icebreaker

Very Okanagan, approachable, not overly sweet, and yes, this one can be a cocktail.

A little peach, a little sparkle, and enough personality to start the conversation for you.

Peach, lemon, soda, and a little optional trouble.

Ingredients

  • Vodka or gin
  • Peach juice or peach nectar
  • Lemon juice
  • Honey syrup
  • Soda water
  • Ice cubes
  • Peach slice or lemon for garnish

Instructions

  1. Fill a shaker or glass with ice.
  2. Add 1.5 ounces vodka or gin.
  3. Add 2 ounces peach juice or peach nectar.
  4. Add 0.5 ounce lemon juice.
  5. Add 1 teaspoon honey syrup.
  6. Shake or stir.
  7. Top with soda water.
  8. Garnish with a peach slice or lemon.

Mocktail option: Skip the vodka or gin and add a little extra soda water.

Nanaimo

Where business meets creativity and nature.

For clients who want their meeting to feel coastal, connected, and a little less predictable. Nanaimo brings oceanfront hosting, creative venue potential, and access that helps make the destination easier to say yes to.

'Ferry' Refreshing

Coastal, refreshing, and extremely not beige.

Sparkling, tropical, and not trying to be another sad conference water station.

Pineapple, lime, sparkle, mint, and coastal main character energy.

Ingredients

  • Sparkling water or soda
  • Pineapple juice
  • Lime juice
  • Splash of coconut milk or coconut water
  • Fresh mint
  • Ice cubes

Instructions

  1. Fill a glass with ice.
  2. Add 2 ounces pineapple juice.
  3. Add 1 ounce lime juice.
  4. Top with sparkling water or soda.
  5. Add a small splash of coconut milk or coconut water.
  6. Garnish with fresh mint.
